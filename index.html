About PhotoDoc Pro

PhotoDoc Pro is a mobile application intended to help users prepare passport-size and ID photos and perform supported photo and document tasks from their device.

The features available to you depend on the version of the app installed on your device. Please review Android permission prompts and grant access only when needed for a feature you choose to use.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the PhotoDoc Pro mobile application (“the App”). Please read it carefully.

1. Information and files you choose to use

The App may request access to photos, media, camera, or documents when a feature requires it. Content you select or capture is used to provide the feature you requested. Android permissions can be managed in your device settings.

2. Storage and processing

How selected files are stored or processed can depend on the feature and app version. Do not use the App to process information you are not authorised to use. If a feature sends information to an online service, the applicable in-app notice or permission flow should explain that processing.

3. Advertising and third-party services

The App may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners, which may include InMobi. Advertising partners may process information such as device or advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from network information, and ad interaction or diagnostic data, subject to their own policies and applicable law. The partners and data used can vary by app version, region, and settings.

For details about a specific advertising partner, please review that partner’s privacy policy and the privacy choices available on your device.

4. Sharing and sale of personal information

We do not sell your personal photos or documents. Information may be processed by service providers when needed to operate app features, deliver advertisements, maintain security, diagnose technical issues, or comply with legal obligations. Actual processing depends on the features and third-party services enabled in the version you use.

5. Data security and retention

Reasonable measures are intended to be used to protect information, but no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Retention depends on where information is processed and the feature involved. You can remove local files using your device’s file-management tools.

6. Children’s privacy

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so the concern can be reviewed.

7. Your choices

  • Manage camera, photo, media, and file permissions in Android settings.
  • Use your device’s advertising settings to reset or limit personalised advertising where those options are available.
  • Choose not to provide optional permissions; some features may then be unavailable.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the App or its services change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us using the email address in the Contact section below.

Important: This policy should match the App’s actual permissions, file handling, analytics, and advertising SDK configuration. Check it against the final app build before submitting it to an advertising network or app store.
